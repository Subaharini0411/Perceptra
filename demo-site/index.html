🚀 Active & Historical Space Missions

Active Operation

Chandrayaan-3 Mission

Lunar exploration mission demonstrating successful soft landing on the Moon's South Pole with Vikram Lander and Pragyan Rover conducting surface in-situ experiments.

Target: Lunar South Pole Status: Mission Accomplished
Halo Orbit

Aditya-L1 Solar Observatory

India's first space-based observatory class solar mission positioned at Sun-Earth Lagrange point L1 to continuously monitor solar corona and coronal mass ejections.

Orbit: Sun-Earth L1 Payloads: 7 Scientific Payloads
Crewed Spaceflight

Gaganyaan Human Flight

Indian human spaceflight programme demonstrating human spaceflight capability to Low Earth Orbit (LEO) with safe return of astronauts to Indian territorial waters.

Target: 400 km LEO Crew: Indian Astronauts
Interplanetary

Mars Orbiter Mission (Mangalyaan)

Historic interplanetary mission placing India as the first Asian nation to reach Martian orbit and the first in the world to do so in its maiden attempt.

Target: Mars Orbit Launch: PSLV-C25
X-Ray Polarimetry

XPoSat Observatory

Dedicated satellite to study polarisation of cosmic X-rays from astronomical sources like black hole X-ray binaries, pulsar wind nebulae, and supernova remnants.

Orbit: 650 km LEO Payload: POLIX & XSPECT
Earth Observation

NISAR Dual-Band Radar

Joint NASA-ISRO Synthetic Aperture Radar observatory utilizing L-band and S-band radar to measure Earth's changing ecosystems, glaciers, and crustal deformation.

Orbit: 747 km Sun-Synchronous Radar: Dual Freq L & S Band
