Chandrayaan-3

India's third lunar mission — successfully landed near the Moon's south pole on August 23, 2023. A historic achievement making India the 4th country to land on the Moon.

🎯 Mission Objectives

  • ✓ Demonstrate safe and soft landing on lunar surface
  • ✓ Deploy Pragyan rover for in-situ scientific experiments
  • ✓ Conduct scientific experiments on lunar surface
  • ✓ Study lunar south pole regolith composition
  • ✓ Detect Sulphur and other elements on lunar south pole

🛸 Spacecraft Components

Vikram Lander
Main lander module with scientific instruments. Soft-landed at 69.37°S latitude.
Pragyan Rover
6-wheeled rover that traversed 100 metres on lunar surface. Carried APXS and LIBS.
Propulsion Module
Carried lander to lunar orbit. Also conducted SHAPE experiment from orbit.
