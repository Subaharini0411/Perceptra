About ISRO

The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) is the space agency of India. It is headquartered in Bengaluru and has been responsible for building and launching satellites, launch vehicles, and developing space technology.

🚀 About ISRO

Founded in 1969, ISRO has grown to become one of the world's leading space agencies. With missions to the Moon, Mars, and the Sun, ISRO continues to push the boundaries of space exploration with cost-effective, indigenous technology.
